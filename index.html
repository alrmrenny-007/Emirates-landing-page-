Frequently asked questions

Quick answers before you book. Reach our contact centre for anything more specific.

What's the checked baggage allowance?

Economy allows 30kg (or two pieces on the US routes), Business 40kg, and First 50kg, depending on route. Skywards Silver, Gold, and Platinum members receive an extra allowance on top of the fare-based limit.

Do Skywards miles expire?

Blue tier miles are valid for three years from the date they're earned. Silver, Gold, and Platinum members keep their miles for as long as they remain active in the program.

How early should I check in?

Online check-in opens 48 hours before departure. At the airport, counters close 60 minutes before departure for most routes and 75 minutes for US-bound flights.

What's on ICE entertainment?

Over 6,500 channels of movies, TV, music, and games, available on every seatback screen fleet-wide, plus live TV and moving-map flight tracking on most aircraft.

Can I choose my seat in advance?

Yes — standard seat selection is free from 48 hours before departure. Preferred and extra-legroom seats can be selected earlier for a fee, or free for Gold and Platinum Skywards members.
